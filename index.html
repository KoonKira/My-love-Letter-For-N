Our Precious Memories

Little moments, big memories.

Here are some of my favorite memories with you.

01

My First Steps♥

April 20, 2025

Back when I was secretly stalking your social media, I had no idea that one day you’d become someone so incredibly special to me. Looking at your posts felt so ordinary back then, but now I smile knowing that the girl I admired from a distance is the one who means so much to my heart.

02

The day I miss♥

November 2, 2025

I still remember when we had planned to go out together, and I was looking forward to that day so much. But then your family suddenly decided to take a trip to Pyin Oo Lwin, and you had to go with them. I know you even argued with your family because you didn’t want to miss seeing me, and that honestly meant more to me than you know. Even though we couldn’t be together that day, this photo reminds me of you and that little story behind it. It wasn’t the day we had planned, but somehow it still became a memory I’ll always treasure.

03

When Noodles Meant Eyerything ♥

May 22, 2026

This was taken during our date at Capital Shopping Center—the day someone got adorably grumpy because we couldn’t have noodle soup. I still can’t help but smile whenever I think about it. Even your little pout was the cutest thing ever. Looking back now, I realize it was never really about the noodles; it was about spending those little moments together. And somehow, even your tiny sulks became some of my favorite memories with you.

04

My Safe Side♥

June 12, 2026

As long as I’m holding your hand, I’m not afraid of anything anymore. Somehow, your hand gives me a kind of peace and courage that nothing else ever could. No matter what life throws at us, as long as we’re together, I know we’ll make it through.

05

The Mini Version of NangHtetThiriNaing♥

December 14, 2025

The first time you showed me your childhood photo, I couldn’t believe the little version of you could be that unbelievably adorable. I just sat there thinking, “How could someone be this cute even as a kid?” And… I have a tiny confession. I still secretly look at that photo at night sometimes, and yes, I even steal a little kiss from it. It always makes me smile and reminds me how lucky I am to love the same adorable girl—just all grown up now. ❤️

06

My Troublemaker!♥

August 03, 2023

Lately, every time I try to hold your hand, you always tease me by sticking up your middle finger instead.You’re such a mischievous little troublemaker. But somehow, even your playful teasing makes me laugh. That’s just so you—always finding a way to make our moments together fun. No matter how cheeky you are, I still end up smiling every single time. You’re my favorite little menace, and I wouldn’t have you any other way.

07

Our Future Home♥

December 18, 2025

This is the little home I imagine us living in together someday. It may not be big or fancy, but as long as it’s filled with your laughter, our love, and the memories we create, it’ll always feel like the most beautiful place in the world. And maybe one day, if we’re lucky enough to have a house full of little ones running around, we’ll laugh and realize we’ve finally outgrown this tiny home. That’s when we’ll move into a bigger one—carrying all the love and memories we built here with us.

08

Silly Moments♥

May 05, 2026

This was the “X-ray” of our first baby… or at least, that’s what we thought. The funniest part? It turned out there were actually two little puppies instead! That’s when I realized I’d somehow ended up marrying the cutest little troublemaker… who might secretly be a puppy in disguise. 🤭 Don’t worry, I’m only teasing. No matter how much I joke around, you’ll always be my favorite girl—the one I love more than anything.
